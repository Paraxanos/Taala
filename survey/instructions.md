# Participant instructions

Shown after consent, immediately before the word list.

> **Do not add romanization examples to this page.** Showing even one, such as a word spelled with `aa`, pushes everyone toward that spelling and destroys the variation the survey exists to measure. The format rules below deliberately use no Hindi words.

---

## How to fill this in

You will see Hindi words one at a time, written in Devanagari.

**For each word, type how you would normally write it using English letters** — the way you would type it in a message to a friend.

There is no correct answer. We want your natural spelling, not a formal or official one.

**Please:**
- use only the letters **a–z**
- use lowercase
- no spaces, numbers or punctuation
- one word per box

**If a second spelling feels equally natural to you**, type it in the "another way I might write it" box. Leave that box empty if only one spelling feels right.

**If you don't know a word**, tick **"don't know this word"** and move on. Please don't guess.

**Please work on your own and don't discuss the words with anyone**, including people sitting next to you. If two people talk about a word, both answers become unusable for us.

There are about 100 words, and it takes about 10 minutes. You can stop at any point.
