## How people write Hindi words in English letters

**What this is.** We are four students running a research project on password security. We are studying how people write Hindi words using English letters.

**What you will do.** You will see about 100 Hindi words in Devanagari script. For each one, type how you would normally write it using English letters. It takes about 10 minutes.

**We do not ask for any password.** Please do not type a password, or part of a password, anywhere in this survey.

**What we collect.** Your spellings, and a few general questions: your first language, which other Indian languages you read, your home state or region, how often you type Hindi in English letters, and your age range.

**What we do not collect.** Your name, roll number, email address or phone number. We do not ask you to sign in. We have no way to connect your answers to you.

**Voluntary.** You can stop at any time and skip any word. There is a "don't know this word" option.

**Because responses are anonymous, we cannot remove your answers after you submit them.** We have no way to find which response was yours.

**[open dataset]** The cleaned spellings may be published as an open dataset under a CC BY 4.0 licence, so that other researchers can use them. The published data contains only the spellings and the general questions above. It contains nothing that could identify you.

**Results.** Overall results will appear in our course report and may appear in a research paper.

**Who to contact.** [supervisor name], [department], [email].

**Eligibility.** You must be 18 or older, and you must be able to read Devanagari script.
