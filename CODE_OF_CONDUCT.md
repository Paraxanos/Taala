# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

- Be respectful. Disagree with code and ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language, including about languages,
  scripts, regions or spelling habits. Many ways of writing Hindi in Latin letters are
  valid; that is the point of the project.
- Do not post other people's passwords or personal data, ever. Use made-up examples.
- Assume good faith, and keep criticism specific and actionable.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these
rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting (Security tab) or contact the maintainer, Paraxanos, through the
profile linked from the repository.
